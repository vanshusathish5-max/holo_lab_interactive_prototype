Structure Overview

Light passes through the cornea, enters through the pupil, gets focused by the biconvex lens, and projects a inverted image onto the retina.

Local Speech Guidance

The human eye is roughly spherical. The outer coat is white and tough (sclera). The transparent front portion is the cornea, which converges incoming light rays toward the lens.
